# Checkout components

This component will have 3 items:
- 1 x Pro Display XDR - Standard glass (£4,599.00)
- 1 x 14-inch MacBook Pro - Space Black (£1,999.00)
- 1 x Magic Keyboard with Touch ID and Numeric Keypad (£199.00)
- 1 x Magic Trackpad (£149.00)

UI for each item should be a image, title, metadata, quantity, remove and price.

You should be able to update the quantity. This needs to show a formatted price, a total price and the ability to remove an item.
You can also add 1 of 3 coupon codes:
- SUMMER10 for 10% off
- WELCOME5 for 5% off
- MEGADEAL20 for 20% off

Total price will include a shipping charge of £8.99. It will also show the tax breakdown below the price (Includes VAT of £x)

When clicking pay, show an alert with the total price paid.
